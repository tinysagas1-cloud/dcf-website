Me Myself & I — Your Witness  |  Last updated: November 2026

Plain language: This app is free. It helps you document evidence safely. It is not legal advice, and it is not an emergency service. These terms explain what you can expect from us and what we ask of you.

What this app is

Me Myself & I — Your Witness is a free evidence-documentation app built by Digital Compass Foundation, a Charitable Incorporated Organisation registered in England and Wales. It helps people experiencing domestic abuse, sexual violence, stalking, harassment, coercive control, and other forms of abuse to safely record and preserve evidence on their own device.

What this app is not

This app is not a substitute for legal advice. It does not tell you what to do with your evidence, whether your evidence is admissible in court, or whether you should report. Always seek advice from a qualified legal professional or support service.

This app is not an emergency service. If you are in immediate danger, call 999 (UK), 911 (US/Canada), 000 (Australia), or your local emergency number. The app does not monitor for emergencies and cannot dispatch help.

The app does not provide counselling, therapy, or emotional support services. If you need someone to talk to, please contact a specialist support service such as the National Domestic Violence Hotline (1-800-799-7233 in the US), Refuge (0808 2000 247 in the UK), or 1800RESPECT (1800 737 732 in Australia).

Cost

Me Myself & I is free for survivors. There is no subscription, no trial period, no premium tier, and no in-app purchases for core functionality. The app is funded by grants and charitable donations. We will never charge survivors to use the app.

Professional access (for lawyers, social workers, police, and support organisations) may carry a fee in the future to sustain the service. Any professional pricing will be published separately and will never affect the free survivor experience.

The calculator disguise

The app appears on your device as "Calc+" — a working calculator. This is a safety feature, not a deception. If your device is monitored by an abuser, the app is designed to be undetectable. The calculator is fully functional. You access the evidence features through a specific sequence within the calculator. We mention this here so you understand why the app looks the way it does.

Your evidence

Your evidence belongs to you. We do not own it, we cannot access it, and we make no claim to it. Everything you record is encrypted on your device with keys that only you hold.

We provide tools to help you organise and preserve your evidence, but we make no guarantee about:

  • Whether your evidence will be admissible in any legal proceeding (this depends on your jurisdiction and circumstances)
  • Whether timestamps or metadata will be accepted by any court or authority
  • The completeness or accuracy of any AI-generated transcriptions

We do our best to maintain the integrity of your evidence through cryptographic hashing, independent timestamps, and provenance labelling (marking whether evidence was captured on-device, reported by you, or exported from a third party). However, the legal weight of digital evidence varies by jurisdiction.

Evidence integrity

The app labels each piece of evidence with its provenance:

  • [device-captured] — recorded directly by the app using your device's camera, microphone, or sensors at the time of the incident
  • [user-reported] — typed or described by you after the fact
  • [user-exported, third-party] — imported from another source (such as screenshots, exported location history, or health data)

These labels are sealed into each evidence item's cryptographic hash and cannot be changed after the fact. They help professionals understand the nature of each piece of evidence.

Your responsibilities

By using this app, you agree to:

  • Use the app for its intended purpose: documenting evidence of abuse or harm you have experienced
  • Not use the app to fabricate evidence, make false accusations, or harass others
  • Keep your 24-word recovery mnemonic safe — if you lose it and have no cloud backup or trusted contact shares, your evidence cannot be recovered by anyone, including us
  • Understand that sharing your evidence (via QR code and decryption key) is your decision and your responsibility

What we ask you not to do

  • Do not reverse-engineer, decompile, or attempt to extract the source code of the app
  • Do not use the app to store or transmit illegal content unrelated to evidence documentation
  • Do not attempt to interfere with the app's encryption, timestamp, or hashing mechanisms
  • Do not impersonate Digital Compass Foundation or misrepresent your affiliation with us

Health data

If you choose to attach health data (from Apple Health or Google Health Connect) to an incident, you are granting the app read-only access to specific data types for a specific time window. This data is encrypted on your device and sealed into your evidence. We never see it. You choose per incident whether to include health data, and you can revoke the app's health data permission at any time through your device settings.

AI features

The app uses AI in two ways:

  • On-device transcription (OpenAI Whisper, running locally) — converts your voice recordings to text without sending audio anywhere. Transcriptions may contain errors. Always review transcriptions before sharing evidence.
  • Guided follow-up questions (planned, using Anthropic Claude API) — will help you document incidents more thoroughly by asking relevant follow-up questions. Only PII-stripped transcripts will be sent. This feature will be clearly labelled and optional.

AI-generated content (transcriptions, suggested questions) is provided as an aid, not as a substitute for your own account of events.

Availability and support

We aim to keep the app available and working, but we cannot guarantee uninterrupted service. The app is designed to work offline — evidence capture, encryption, and local storage work without an internet connection. Cloud backup and timestamp verification require connectivity.

We are a small charity. We provide support on a best-efforts basis. For help, contact support@digitalcompassfoundation.org.

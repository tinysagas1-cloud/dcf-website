What makes it court-grade

Three layers of forensic integrity

Encryption

AES-256-GCM with keys derived from the user's recovery mnemonic via Argon2id + HKDF. Each evidence item encrypted individually on capture.

Hash chain

SHA-256 chain links every entry. If any single entry is modified, every subsequent hash breaks — mathematical proof of tampering or integrity.

Timestamps

Two separate RFC 3161 timestamp authorities, hardcoded. Independent of the device clock. Proves when evidence was created, not when the user says it was.

Unique in the market

The interaction timestamp log

Every user interaction during evidence capture is logged — when each question was presented, when they began responding, duration of pauses, when they submitted each answer. Chained and timestamped alongside the evidence itself.

This defeats the coaching allegation — the most common challenge to self-documented evidence. No competitor offers this.

Evidence intelligence

Your phone already knows more than you think

The app pulls corroborating data from sources the user may not even realise they have.

Health data integration

Apple Health / Google Health Connect — heart rate, sleep, steps, blood oxygen, ambient noise (Apple Watch). 2 hours before → 4 hours after each incident. Opt-in per incident.

GPS on everything

Every piece of evidence — voice, photo, video, text — automatically tagged with lat/long/accuracy/altitude/source. The app knows where you are when you record.

Photo EXIF extraction

Import a photo from your gallery and the app extracts the GPS coordinates and timestamp already embedded in it. That photo from three weeks ago already has its location data.

Location history guide

Step-by-step guide to export Google Timeline / Apple Significant Locations. The user's own phone has been tracking where they've been — powerful third-party corroboration.

Provenance labelling

Every piece of evidence tagged: [device-captured] vs [user-reported] vs [user-exported, third-party]. Courts see exactly where each piece came from and how it was obtained.

Self-reported location

'Where did this happen?' — map, address, or text. For recording from a different location after the fact. Clearly labelled as self-reported, separate from device GPS.

Three fail-safe guarantees

Evidence never lost. Disguise never breaks. Always a path.

Evidence survives

24-word recovery mnemonic. Shamir 2-of-3 key splitting across trusted contacts. Encrypted cloud backup with per-country data residency. Emergency offline export.

Disguise holds

App crash, OS force-stop, low battery shutdown, mid-recording interruption — every failure state returns to the calculator. Passes the stranger test.

Always a path

Phone destroyed? Cloud backup. No internet? Offline export via AirDrop/Bluetooth/USB. Contacts compromised? Any two of three reconstruct the key.
